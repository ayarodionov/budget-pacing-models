## The scenario as simulated

- campaign: B = 840,000 units over 7 days, control period 60 s, pacing goal: follow the traffic
- traffic: λ = 1000 requests/s, charged share r = 0.1, profile: varies; the center's belief: the true profile
- spend delay: measured distribution (longest 240 s); the center weights by the distribution
- delivery delay of p: 0 s (planned for 0 s)
- 1000 runs per model, seed 1

**Notes:**

- token cap (b) counts each grant for the longest delay (240 s): its guarantee needs a hard maximum
